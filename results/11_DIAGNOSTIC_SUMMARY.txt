DIAGNOSTIC ANALYSIS - NEGATIVE RAINFALL CORRELATION
================================================================================

Problem: Rainfall shows negative correlation with yield despite expected positive effect

Root Causes Identified:
1. Multicollinearity with temperature (model attributes rain signal to temp)
2. Non-linear/threshold effects not captured by linear correlations  
3. Feature scaling/normalization may be suboptimal
4. Raw RH2M humidity less meaningful than VPD

Solutions Tested:
1. VPD derivation: +0.062 improvement over RH2M (marginal but positive)
2. Interaction terms: +0.06 improvement (Temp x Precip, Temp x Humidity)
3. Polynomial terms: No improvement
4. Stress metrics: Weak signals

Top Recommendations:
1. Add Temp x Precipitation interaction (expected +0.06 signal)
2. Replace RH2M with VPD (+0.03 improvement)
3. Implement crop-stage masking for rainfall (+2-5% R² expected)
4. Consider separate wet/dry season models (+3-5% R² expected)

Next Step: Retrain model with interactions + VPD, validate on test set
